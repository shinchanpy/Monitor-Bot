# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 13, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Monitor Bot** ("the Bot") may collect, use, store, and process information when you use the Bot on Discord.

## 1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, the Bot may process information associated with your Discord account or server, such as:

- Discord User ID.
- Discord Server/Guild ID.
- Discord Channel ID.
- Discord Role ID.
- Discord username or display name.
- Command usage information.
- Information provided directly through Bot commands.
- Configuration information required for server features.

Not every type of information is necessarily collected from every user.

## 2. Information Provided Through Commands

Some Bot features may require you to provide information through Discord commands or interactions.

Information submitted through these features may be processed to provide the requested functionality.

You should not submit passwords, authentication tokens, payment information, or other highly sensitive information to the Bot.

## 3. Third-Party Services

Some features of the Bot may interact with third-party services or APIs.

When such features are used, information necessary to perform the requested operation may be transmitted to the relevant third-party service.

Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

## 4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Provide and operate Bot features.
- Process commands and requests.
- Maintain server configurations.
- Provide verification or profile-related functionality.
- Prevent abuse and unauthorized use.
- Troubleshoot technical problems.
- Maintain and improve the Bot.
- Maintain security and reliability.

Information will not be used for purposes unrelated to operating the Bot unless permitted by applicable law or otherwise disclosed.

## 5. Data Storage

Some information may be stored in databases or other infrastructure used to operate the Bot.

The Bot may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide its functionality, maintain security, comply with applicable obligations, or resolve disputes.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the feature involved.

## 6. Data Security

Reasonable measures may be used to protect information processed by the Bot.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

You acknowledge that information transmitted through Discord and other online services may be subject to risks outside the Bot owner's control.

## 7. Data Sharing

We do not intentionally sell personal information.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

- Operate the Bot.
- Use required third-party services.
- Protect the Bot, its users, or infrastructure.
- Prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
- Comply with applicable laws or legal requests.

## 8. Discord Information

The Bot operates through Discord. Discord may independently collect and process information according to its own policies.

For information about how Discord handles your data, please review Discord's applicable privacy documentation.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Users should comply with Discord's age requirements and applicable laws when using Discord and the Bot.

## 10. Your Choices

You can generally stop the Bot from processing further information by:

- Removing the Bot from a server you control.
- Stopping use of the Bot.
- Avoiding optional commands or features that require additional information.

If you have a data-related request, contact the Bot owner through the official support/contact method associated with Monitor Bot.

## 11. Data Deletion Requests

If you believe the Bot has stored information associated with you and you would like it deleted, you may contact the Bot owner with sufficient information to identify the relevant data.

Some information may need to be retained when required for security, legal, or legitimate operational purposes.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

The "Last Updated" date will be changed when appropriate. Continued use of the Bot after an update means you acknowledge the updated Privacy Policy.

## 13. Contact

For privacy questions, data deletion requests, or other concerns, contact the Bot owner through the official support/contact method associated with Monitor Bot.
